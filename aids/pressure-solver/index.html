The pressure solver, cell by cell

Every interval, a fluid simulation solves for the one pressure field that leaves every cell exactly full once things move. This board runs a small solver implementation and stops wherever you like: one interval, one Newton step, one multigrid sweep, or one cell. yellow is what the step writes and blue, dashed is what it reads.

      A teaching implementation of a nonlinear pressure solver, on a 16×16 grid of 1 mm cells. It runs the full structure of a production solve: frozen enthalpy, a local-equilibrium source, backward-Euler face kicks, a 5-point Jacobian, BiCGStab with an aggregation V-cycle, a backtracking line search, and adaptive intervals that halve dt on failure. It leaves out heat recovery, capillarity and viscosity (a uniform drag stands in for it), and fallback solvers.
